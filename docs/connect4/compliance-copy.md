# Connect 4 Synchronous Staked Play — Compliance Copy (Phase 0)

Approved copy blocks for create/join/ready UX surfaces.

## Create Challenge Disclosure

> This is a live, synchronous match for real stakes. Your stake is escrowed when you create a challenge. The challenge expires if no opponent joins within **5 minutes**.

> Once an opponent joins, both players must tap Ready within **30 seconds**. If both players are not ready in time, the challenge is canceled and both players are refunded.

## Join Challenge Disclosure

> By accepting this challenge, your stake is escrowed immediately. This is a live match with strict server-enforced timers.

> Each turn has a **30-second** deadline. Missing your turn deadline results in forfeiture and loss of the full pot.

## Ready Room Disclosure

> Match start requires both players to be Ready before the countdown ends. Server timers are authoritative.

> Connection interruptions do not pause the game clock. Reconnect indicators may appear for up to **10 seconds**, but turn deadlines still apply.

## Result/Forfeit Disclosure

> Win: winner receives the full pot.

> Draw: both players are refunded their own stake.

> Forfeit (including turn timeout): non-forfeiting player receives the full pot.
